First credential · highest immediate relevance

SnowPro Core COF-C03

100 questions · 115 minutes · $175

Architecture, administration, ingestion, data types, performance, governance, collaboration, protection, and connectivity.

Personal advantage: SQL, pipelines, analytics consumers, production support, and Snowflake exposure are already familiar. Concentrate on exact platform behavior, privileges, cost controls, and newer C03 features.

Six-module plan

Capstone evidence

Load a bounded Discogs slice into raw, staging, and analytics layers; apply least-privilege roles; isolate warehouses; profile queries; document cost; and demonstrate governance, sharing, retention, and recovery.
